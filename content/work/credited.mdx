## Overview

Streaming apps make it easy to find an artist and almost impossible to find who actually wrote, produced, or engineered a track. Credited fixes that: identify a song, and see the full story behind it, performers, songwriters, producers, and everyone else who made it.

We led product on Credited, owning research, strategy, delivery, and QA, working alongside the founder, a design lead, and four engineers.

## The challenge

Credits are often missing, incomplete, or wrong, so the people who made a song struggle to get recognised for it, and listeners curious about who's behind a track have nowhere good to look. The guiding question: how do you make the full story behind a song easy to discover?

## Our role

We ran product on Credited end to end: research, strategy, user flows, delivery, QA, and launch, working closely with the founder, a design lead, and four engineers through 2025.

We started with research: around 15 to 20 artists, producers, and listeners took part in interviews, surveys, and usability sessions. The findings shaped the product directly: credits were often incomplete, people wanted faster song identification, they wanted context before leaving the app, and they used different streaming services, so Credited needed to work with more than one.

## Our approach

Research turned into a clear discovery journey: identify a song, listen, explore its credits, discover its contributors, and save it. From there, we planned an admin tool so Credited's own team could manage and correct credit data.

The hardest problem was song recognition using just one device, instead of needing a second device playing the song nearby. Early versions were inconsistent, so we tested repeatedly across a wide range of songs, Nigerian and international, until it worked reliably.

We turned every requirement into user flows and Jira tickets, reviewed in stand-ups with design and engineering, and covered by test cases before release. With no dedicated QA person, quality was everyone's job.

## The solution

Identify a song, see who made it, listen, and go deeper if you want to.

- **Song identification** works by listening, Shazam-style, or by searching directly.
- **Credits** break down performers, songwriters, composers, and other contributors, each with their own name attached.
- **Listening** includes a short in-app preview, with a direct link to continue on Spotify or Apple Music.
- **Samples** show where a song pulled from, linking current tracks back to their originals.
- **Saved history** lets signed-in users keep track of everything they've identified; guests can still identify and view credits, just without saving.
- **An admin tool** lets the team manage users and correct song credits directly, with role-based access for Admins and Super Admins.

## Key features

- **Full credits, not just the artist.** Performers, songwriters, composers, producers, and engineers, all named.
- **One-tap song ID.** Listen and identify, no typing required.
- **Same-device recognition.** No second device needed, a real technical challenge we solved through repeated testing.
- **Keep listening elsewhere.** A clean handoff to Spotify or Apple Music.
- **Sample discovery.** See what a song sampled, and where that came from.
- **Guest access.** Identify songs and view credits without creating an account.
- **An internal credit-management tool.** Built for the team to keep data accurate as the catalogue grows.

## Branding

Credited's look is warm and editorial: a deep gold as the primary colour, paired with a warm cream background in light mode and a near-black background in dark mode, both fully supported and switching automatically with the device's system setting. The logo, a stylised disc inside a "C," carries through the app icon, the admin dashboard, and the identification screen's central button.

## Built by AloTech Labs

Credited is a native app with light and dark mode built in from the start, each with its own colour set rather than a simple invert. Behind the identification feature sits custom work to make single-device song recognition reliable, tested repeatedly across a wide range of real songs before it shipped. An internal admin dashboard, with role-based access for Admins and Super Admins, gives the team direct control over song credit data as the catalogue grows.

## Outcome

Credited is live on the App Store. After a round of iteration based on real usage, onboarding completion and activation roughly doubled. Credited's team also came away with an internal tool it didn't have before: a working way to keep credit data accurate as the product grows.

## Closing

Credited shows what careful product work can do with a genuinely hard technical problem: keep the experience simple for the user, even when what's happening behind it, recognising a song from one device, pulling together accurate credits, isn't simple at all.
