## Overview

Creators build real audiences, but turning that attention into steady income usually means juggling several platforms: one to post, one to message, another to get paid. Youfanly brings it into one place. Creators share content with subscribers, sell individual posts and media, message their fans, and withdraw what they earn, all from the same dashboard.

We designed Youfanly across web and mobile, from the first sign-up screen to the creator's wallet.

## The challenge

A creator platform has two audiences with different needs. Fans want to discover creators and see what they pay for without friction. Creators need control: who sees each post, what it costs, when it goes out, and exactly what they've earned.

The money side has to be just as clear. Subscriptions, tips, and one-off purchases all arrive in different ways, and creators need to see every one of them and withdraw without guessing what's pending.

The brief: give creators full control over their content and income, in a space that stays simple for fans.

## Our role

We shaped Youfanly from the ground up: product flows and the full interface across web and mobile. That covered the fan side (discovery, subscriptions, purchases, messaging) and the creator side (posting, media, subscription plans, earnings, and withdrawals), plus account, notification, and privacy settings.

## Our approach

We started with the creator's working day: post something, decide who can see it, reply to fans, check what came in. Every main area (Home, Posts, Media, Messages, Subscribers, Earnings) sits in one persistent sidebar on the web and a bottom bar on mobile, so creators can move between them without losing their place.

Control was designed in at every step. Each post has its own permissions and price, scheduling is part of posting, not an extra tool, and paid media in messages stays locked until the fan pays. On the money side, every transaction gets an ID, a type, and a status, so a creator can always trace what came in and what's still pending.

## The solution

Post, set who sees it, message fans, grow subscribers, and get paid.

- **Posting** lets creators publish to their feed or schedule for later, with a price per post when they want one.
- **Post permissions** decide who can see and reply: subscribers only, or only people who've bought that post.
- **The media library** keeps photos and videos in folders, ready to attach to any post or message.
- **Messages** support paid media: a creator sets a price, and the fan sees the content once they pay.
- **Subscription plans** come in tiers, each with its own benefits, and discounts for longer durations. Plans can be edited or switched off at any time.
- **Earnings** shows the available and pending balance, totals for subscriptions, tips, and purchases, and a full transaction history.
- **Withdrawals** go to a bank account or a crypto wallet (USDT), with the minimum and exchange rate shown before submitting.

## Key features

- **Every post, its own rules.** Price, audience, and timing set per post.
- **Paid media in messages.** Content stays locked until the fan pays.
- **Subscription tiers.** Different benefits per tier, with discounts for longer plans.
- **One media library.** Folders for photos and videos, reusable across posts and messages.
- **Clear earnings.** Subscriptions, tips, and purchases tracked separately, with a status on every transaction.
- **Flexible withdrawals.** Bank account or USDT wallet.
- **Notifications that sort themselves.** Comments, mentions, likes, tags, and new subscribers, each with its own filter.
- **Web and mobile, one product.** The same tools on both, laid out for each screen.

## Branding

Youfanly is warm and creator-first: a deep plum as the primary colour, soft pink surfaces, and a lightning-bolt wordmark with "For Creators" set beneath it. The interface stays light and quiet so creators' photos and videos carry the colour, with plum kept for the actions that matter: posting, subscribing, and withdrawing.
