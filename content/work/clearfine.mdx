## Overview

Parking fines and road charges are easy to lose track of. A notice turns up on a windscreen or in the post, the deadline is somewhere you'll probably forget, and missing it usually costs more. Clearfine gives UK drivers one place to catch a fine the moment it happens, see what's owed and by when, and get reminded before it's too late.

We built Clearfine end to end: product strategy, design, brand, and development, taking it from a blank page to a live App Store app in two months.

## The challenge

Every council and charging authority has its own portal, deadlines, and early-payment discounts. Drivers were relying on memory, paper, or a calendar entry they might forget, and anyone with more than one car had no single view of what they owed.

The brief was simple: make it fast to capture a fine, make the deadline impossible to miss, and keep everything organised by vehicle.

## Our role

We built Clearfine from the ground up: product, design, brand, and engineering, across a two-month build to launch. Before design started, we spoke to drivers about how they currently track fines and looked at what was already on the market. The clear finding: every existing app solved part of the problem, not the whole thing.

## Our approach

We planned Clearfine as a full product, not just a design exercise, breaking the build into clear stages so design and engineering worked from the same plan throughout.

The goal for the design was calm, not alarming. A product dealing with fines and deadlines shouldn't add to the stress, so we kept the visual language light and reassuring, used colour sparingly, and built onboarding that explains why each step helps.

We ran our own QA across every part of the app before release, so nothing shipped untested.

## The solution

Add a vehicle, capture a fine, track its status, get reminded, then pay.

- **Onboarding** is quick: sign up with email or Apple ID, verify, and turn on the reminders you want.
- **Fine capture** works two ways: scan a photo for automatic detection, or enter it manually.
- **Vehicles** auto-fill from a number plate via DVLA lookup, so there's barely any typing.
- **The dashboard** shows every fine's status at a glance: Pending, Overdue, or Paid.
- **Charges and fines** show everything needed to act, with a direct link to pay.
- **Notifications** nudge at the right moment, without turning into noise.

## Key features

- **Scan a fine, done.** Point a camera at a notice and Clearfine fills in the details.
- **DVLA vehicle lookup.** Enter a reg number and the make, colour, and year fill in automatically.
- **MOT and road tax reminders.** Clearfine tracks these alongside fines, so nothing lapses quietly.
- **Multiple vehicles, one app.** Every car keeps its own fine history.
- **Clear status, always.** Pending, Overdue, or Paid, visible without opening a record.
- **Reminders that matter.** Alerts before a deadline and when something's overdue.
- **Straight to the council.** No fake in-app payment, just a clean handoff to pay.
- **Basic and Premium.** Multiple vehicles sit behind Premium, with a free trial.

## Branding

Clearfine's look is built around calm clarity: white, black, and one primary teal, used sparingly so it stays meaningful. Headings use Cabinet Grotesk, body text uses Lexend, giving the product an editorial, considered feel. Real UK council logos appear throughout, grounding the app in the everyday reality of UK parking, not a generic fintech look.

Teal never covers more than a small part of any screen, every view has one clear primary action, and the whole system runs off a small, consistent set of colours and tokens.

## Built by AloTech Labs

Clearfine is a native iOS app, backed by our own image-detection service for fine scanning and a DVLA integration for vehicle lookup. We built the two in parallel, so capture and review could be tested together early. Every feature area went through its own QA pass before release.

## Outcome

Clearfine shipped on the App Store around two months after work began, with more than 80 screens built across the full journey: onboarding, scanning, vehicles, fines, reminders, and subscriptions. It's live today.

## Closing

Clearfine shows what we do best: take a genuinely frustrating everyday problem and build something around it that feels calm instead of stressful, from the first idea to a live App Store release.
