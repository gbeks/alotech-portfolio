## Overview

Most dating apps are built for discovery: more profiles, more matches, more swiping. Aligned is built for what happens after that, actually going on dates and working out whether someone's right for you. It's a private space to log who you're seeing, reflect honestly after a date, and keep track of what actually matters to you, without turning dating into a scorecard.

We're building Aligned end to end: product, design, brand, and engineering.

## The challenge

The part of dating that happens after a match tends to live in someone's head, or scattered across notes apps and conversations with friends, with no easy way to look back and spot a pattern.

A competitive review of the dating app space confirmed the gap: plenty of tools for finding people, almost nothing for reflecting honestly on how it's actually going. Aligned was built to fill that gap.

## Our role

We're building Aligned from the ground up, the same end-to-end model we used for Clearfine: product, design, brand, and engineering. Our product team defined exactly how every screen should behave, our design team shaped the visual identity and every flow, and our engineering team is building the app and the systems behind it, including a gentle AI layer with strict rules about what it can and can't do.

## Our approach

Aligned was planned in detail from the start, with every screen and every feature given a clear purpose before design began. That kept design and engineering aligned throughout, instead of resolving questions screen by screen.

The brief was to make Aligned feel like the opposite of a typical dating app: calm, private, and considered, closer to a journal than a social app. That same principle shaped the AI work. Every AI feature follows one hard rule: never score, rank, or conclude anything about a person, only ever offer a gentle, optional observation.

## The solution

Add a connection, log a date, reflect on it, check it against what matters to you, and watch your own patterns build up over time.

- **Journey** is a calendar and journal in one place, showing every date and reflection at a glance.
- **Journaling** supports free writing or video, plus structured reflections after a date.
- **Post-date reflections** walk through how you felt, a few optional prompts, an honest check against what matters to you, and one closing question: do you want to keep seeing this person?
- **The alignment check** reviews your non-negotiables and preferences as Met, Unmet, or Still Deciding, never a score, and every check is saved as a snapshot in time.
- **Profiles** bring everything about one person, their dates, reflections, and alignment history, into one place.
- **Gentle AI support** is opt-in and quiet: a prompt when you seem stuck, a soft rephrasing if you can't find the words, an optional summary, and occasional pattern observations, never a verdict.
- **Privacy** is built in from day one: full data export, and a genuine 30-day delete-and-restore window.
- **One honest plan.** £10.99/month or £104.99/year, everything included, 30-day free trial.

## Key features

- **Reflection, not swiping.** No discovery feed, just a private space to process how dating is actually going.
- **A three-way alignment check.** Met, Unmet, or Still Deciding, never a score, and nothing gets rewritten after the fact.
- **AI with real boundaries.** Every AI feature is optional, dismissible, and built never to score, rank, or name names.
- **Profiles that do the remembering.** Every connection's dates, reflections, and alignment history, all in one place.
- **Everything links itself.** Dates, reflections, and people stay connected automatically, no manual tidying.
- **Full data ownership.** Export everything, delete with a genuine recovery window.
- **One plan, everything included.** No premium upsell on emotional support.

## Branding

Aligned looks nothing like a typical dating app, by design. Navy, warm paper, and a single deep red, an italic wordmark, and a wax-seal monogram as the app icon. The tagline, "Sealed with intent," and the voice, "No scores. No pressure. No judgement," carry through copy as much as colour. Even the launch animation plays into it: an envelope seals shut, a wax seal stamps down, and the wordmark rises beneath it.

Square corners throughout and a serif-led type system give the whole thing the feel of stationery or a personal journal, not another app.

## Built by AloTech Labs

Aligned is a native iOS app built around three connected pieces: the people you're dating, the dates you log, and the reflections you write, plus your personal checklist. The AI layer is carefully staged: quick support while you're writing, a few gentle touches right after you save, and slower pattern-spotting that only kicks in once there's enough history, and it never scores or concludes anything.

Sign-in is passwordless, and subscriptions run entirely through Apple and Google, so Aligned never touches payment details directly.

## Closing

Aligned applies the same end-to-end model we used on Clearfine (product, design, and engineering together) to a very different kind of problem, not a logistics headache, but an emotional one. It's proof our approach holds up well beyond utility apps.
