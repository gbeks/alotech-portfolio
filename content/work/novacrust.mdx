## Overview

For African freelancers, creators, and remote workers, paying for global tools or getting paid by overseas clients shouldn't be the hard part of the job. Novacrust puts it all in one app: hold money in dollars, euros, pounds, or naira, swap between them, and pay anywhere with a virtual card.

We built Novacrust end to end, product, design, and development, across a mobile app and responsive web, taking it from a detailed PRD to a live product. It now has more than 10,000 users.

## The challenge

Traditional banking makes cross-border payments slow, expensive, and unreliable, which makes it harder to subscribe to the tools the work depends on, or get paid by clients abroad.

Conversations with users and a look at the market turned up the same five problems again and again:

- **Verification that felt like paperwork**, and often failed.
- **Few virtual cards that reliably worked** for subscriptions and global tools.
- **Limited ways to fund a card**, with some platforms only taking certain currencies.
- **Fees and exchange rates that only showed up afterwards.**
- **Low trust**, after people had lost money or had cards declined at checkout.

The brief: make moving money across borders feel clear and in control, without cutting corners on compliance.

## Our role

We took Novacrust from requirements to launch: product, design, and engineering under one roof. Novacrust's brand identity and a detailed PRD, covering features, compliance rules, and technical limits, were the starting point. Our product team turned those requirements into clear flows and closed the gaps along the way, our design team shaped every screen across mobile and web, and our engineering team built the app and everything behind it: accounts, verification, cards, wallets, exchange, and transactions.

## Our approach

We started by splitting the requirements into what users see and what the system handles behind the scenes, then marked the moments that make or break trust in a payments product: verification, funding a card, and withdrawing money. From there, we mapped the full journey from first visit to first transaction.

That shaped three questions that guided both the design and the build:

1. How do we simplify verification without cutting corners on compliance?
2. How do we help people feel in control of their money at every step?
3. How do we take the uncertainty out of fees, exchange rates, and failed payments?

Because product, design, and engineering sat in the same team, every flow was reviewed by the engineers who'd build it while it was still a wireframe. Questions about compliance rules, edge cases, and third-party limits were answered before high-fidelity design, not discovered halfway through development.

## The solution

Sign up, verify, create a card, fund it, and pay anywhere.

- **Onboarding** introduces the card and what it's for, from online and in-store payments to tuition through platforms like Flywire and Western Union Education.
- **Sign-up and verification** happen in short steps: basic details, an email code, a six-digit PIN, then address and ID only when they're needed to unlock the card. A separate compliance step upgrades the account with a NIN, a means of ID, and a utility bill.
- **Home** brings accounts, cards, and recent transactions into one view. New users see a clear prompt to create their first card instead of an empty screen.
- **Card creation** lets people name the card, choose Mastercard or Visa, and set a starting amount, with creation and funding fees shown before they commit.
- **The card screen** keeps funding, withdrawing, and freezing right on the card, with card details and billing address one tap from being copied.
- **Exchange and send money** sit one tap from home, with the conversion fee and today's rate shown up front.
- **Every transaction gets a receipt**: amount, fee, status, old and new balance, and a reference, ready to share.

## Key features

- **Verification in small steps.** No single long form; trust builds as people go.
- **Costs before commitment.** Card creation, funding, conversion, and withdrawal fees all shown up front, never as a surprise.
- **Four currencies, one wallet.** Dollars, euros, pounds, and naira, with exchange one tap away.
- **A home screen that shows where money goes.** Balances, cards, and recent transactions together, with a clear first step for new users.
- **Card controls where you need them.** Fund, withdraw, or freeze straight from the card.
- **Receipts for everything.** Clear status, successful or failed, with a reference to share.

## Branding

Novacrust's brand was already in place: a deep teal, soft mint, and a bright, confident green, with a bold, wide display typeface for headlines. Our contribution was the card itself. Since the card is what people see every time they pay, it needed an identity of its own, so we designed a bold green card with a gold chip that runs across the website, onboarding, and the app, plus darker teal versions for the cards inside the app.

## Built by AloTech Labs

Novacrust runs on a mobile app and a responsive web app, sharing one set of accounts, cards, and transactions. Behind the screens, we built the parts a payments product can't get wrong:

- **Identity verification**, with NIN and ID checks connected to a verification partner, plus a tiered compliance step that upgrades an account when more is needed.
- **Virtual card issuing**, so a card can be created, funded, withdrawn from, frozen, and unfrozen in real time.
- **Multi-currency wallets** for dollars, euros, pounds, and naira, with live exchange rates and fees calculated before anything is confirmed.
- **Funding and transfers** through payment partners, with every movement of money recorded as a transaction with a status and a reference.
- **Security throughout**: email verification, a six-digit PIN, and separate password and PIN management.

Every flow was tested end to end before release, including the failure cases (a declined payment, a failed verification, a failed transfer), so people always see a clear status instead of an error.
